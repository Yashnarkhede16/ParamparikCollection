Rooted in Heritage

Born from Tradition, Crafted by Hand

Paramparik Collection began at a small wooden workbench with a simple dream: to preserve the timeless charm of Maharashtrian and Indian heritage jewellery in a fast-paced modern world.

Founded by artisan Neha Borole, every Nath, Bindi, Bangle set, and Jhumka is thoughtfully designed and assembled by hand. Rather than mass-producing pieces, we focus on soulful details — carefully chosen pearls, antique gold-toned filigree, and vibrant festive accents.

Whether you are dressing for a wedding, a festive puja, or simply celebrating your roots, Paramparik Collection brings heirloom warmth to your treasured moments.
